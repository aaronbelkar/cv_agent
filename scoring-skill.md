# Scoring Skill — 100-Point CV Scoring Model

> This is the authoritative scoring model. Always apply the full model. Never simplify to fewer categories.

## Score Formula

**CV Score = Role Fit + Evidence + Ownership + ATS Integrity + Human Scan + Israeli Localization + Trust, minus penalties**
Bounded: 0 to 100.

---

## Category Weights

| Category | Points |
|---|---:|
| Job-specific relevance | 25 |
| Achievement evidence | 20 |
| Ownership and career logic | 15 |
| ATS integrity | 15 |
| Human scan and hierarchy | 10 |
| Israeli localization | 10 |
| Trust and hygiene | 5 |
| **Total** | **100** |

---

## 1. Job-Specific Relevance: 25 points

| Criterion | Points | Full-credit standard |
|---|---:|---|
| Essential requirements | 0-12 | Every mandatory, job-related requirement is visible and supported: core skill, certification, work authorization, language, domain, or location constraint |
| Functional and domain match | 0-6 | Recent work addresses substantially similar problems, customers, systems, or business models. Transferable relevance earns full credit when explicitly translated |
| Recency and depth | 0-4 | Relevant capability appears in recent evidence and at the depth required. A skill named only in the Skills section does not prove proficiency |
| Target clarity | 0-3 | Headline, summary, and recent experience create one coherent role identity without title inflation |

---

## 2. Achievement Evidence: 20 points

| Criterion | Points | Full-credit standard |
|---|---:|---|
| Outcomes and metrics | 0-8 | Most important recent bullets show verified change in revenue, cost, time, quality, risk, adoption, reliability, or mission delivery |
| Causal contribution | 0-5 | Bullets distinguish the candidate's personal action from the team result |
| Scope and baseline | 0-4 | Scale is visible: users, transactions, budget, datasets, team size, cycle time, before/after context |
| Verifiable artifacts | 0-3 | Portfolio, GitHub, shipped product, publication, or work evidence supports major claims |

> Metrics are not mandatory in every bullet. The principle is evidentiary specificity, not numerical decoration. A lawyer shows case complexity. A teacher shows class outcomes. An ops specialist shows throughput and SLA.

---

## 3. Ownership and Career Logic: 15 points

| Criterion | Points | Full-credit standard |
|---|---:|---|
| End-to-end ownership | 0-6 | Shows identification, decision, execution, and follow-through within an accurate scope |
| Problem solving and delivery | 0-4 | Demonstrates obstacles handled, trade-offs made, adaptation, and completion — not a list of assigned duties |
| Influence and collaboration | 0-3 | Shows effective work across functions, clients, vendors, or leadership |
| Progression or sustained mastery | 0-2 | Career history shows broader scope, deeper expertise, or consistently strong contribution. Nonlinear careers receive credit when transitions are explained |

> "Owned" earns nothing alone. It earns points when the CV defines what was owned, the scope of authority, the collaborators, and the result.

---

## 4. ATS Integrity: 15 points

| Criterion | Points | Full-credit standard |
|---|---:|---|
| Parseability | 0-5 | Searchable text, conventional reading order, single column, standard font, no essential information in images, no complex headers/footers/tables |
| Terminology coverage | 0-6 | Truthful coverage of required titles, skills, tools, certifications, and domain terms — including common variants |
| Natural semantic context | 0-2 | Keywords appear in readable accomplishment statements with level and context — not a repeated token cloud |
| Field extraction | 0-2 | Name, contact details, titles, employers, dates, Education, Skills, and Languages appear under standard labels in consistent formats |

---

## 5. Human Scan and Hierarchy: 10 points

| Criterion | Points | Full-credit standard |
|---|---:|---|
| Top-third proposition | 0-4 | Name, target identity, strongest relevant evidence, and core capabilities visible before the lower two-thirds of page one |
| Experience hierarchy | 0-3 | Recent titles, companies, dates, and first bullets easy to compare. Reverse chronology. Gaps not visually confusing |
| Density and readability | 0-3 | Concise bullets, visible section hierarchy, consistent typography, useful white space, no paragraph walls |

---

## 6. Israeli Localization: 10 points

| Criterion | Points | Full-credit standard |
|---|---:|---|
| Language and sector fit | 0-3 | CV language matches the posting, employer, customers, and operating environment |
| Dugri communication | 0-2 | Direct, specific, professional phrasing. No inflated superlatives, excessive hedging, or ceremonial prose |
| Service or equivalent evidence | 0-2 | Relevant military, national, civil, or early-career experience translated into job competencies. Non-service receives no penalty |
| Ecosystem context | 0-2 | Unfamiliar employers, startup stages, or global responsibility briefly contextualized |
| Practical local signal | 0-1 | City/region, work authorization when material, Hebrew/English capability clear without excessive personal data |

---

## 7. Trust and Hygiene: 5 points

| Criterion | Points | Full-credit standard |
|---|---:|---|
| Internal consistency | 0-2 | Titles, dates, education, LinkedIn, and portfolio agree. Metrics are plausible and attributable |
| Language quality | 0-2 | No material spelling, grammar, translation, punctuation, or formatting errors |
| Privacy discipline | 0-1 | Omits unnecessary personal information (birth date, ID number, marital status, photo) while including job-relevant logistics |

---

## Penalties and Score Caps

| Defect | Adjustment |
|---|---|
| Missing or invalid phone/email | Score capped at 40 |
| Image-only or materially unparseable file | -15 and ATS Integrity capped at 3/15 |
| Missing a genuinely essential requirement | Relevant points lost; "not eligible" flag if legally/operationally essential |
| Generic CV with no vacancy-specific alignment | Score capped at 74 |
| Material contradiction across CV and linked profiles | -10 to -25; severe fabrication capped at 39 |
| Keyword stuffing or hidden text | -5 to -15 |
| Unexplained inflated title or ownership claim | -5 to -15 |
| Unnecessary personal data (age, marital status, ID, photo) | -1 to -3 (privacy risk only — never penalize the attribute itself) |

> Penalties must never punish: career gaps, parental leave, reserve duty, disability accommodation, non-service, immigrant status, or an unconventional name. Label unclear evidence as "unknown," not negative.

---

## Score Interpretation

| Score | Interpretation | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 90-100 | Exceptional, sharply targeted, highly credible | Submit after a final factual and privacy check |
| 85-89 | Interview-ready and differentiated | Submit; improve one weak category if competition is strong |
| 75-84 | Competitive but not dominant | Tailor requirements, strengthen recent evidence, improve first-page decision case |
| 65-74 | Plausible but generic or under-evidenced | Rebuild summary and recent bullets around the target role |
| 50-64 | Significant relevance, proof, or parsing problems | Reconstruct content architecture — cosmetic edits will not help |
| 0-49 | Non-converting or unreliable | Fix eligibility, contact info, parseability, or basic chronology before applying |

> A score of 92 in one vacancy can be 68 in another — relevance carries 25 points. Always report both a **Master CV Quality Score** and an **Application Fit Score** separately.

---

## Calibration by Career Stage

| Profile | Evidence that earns credit | Common error |
|---|---|---|
| Student or first role | Projects, internships, service, volunteering, coursework outputs, competitions, portfolio | Penalizing absence of commercial tenure |
| Early career | Concrete delivery, tools in context, speed of learning, increasing independence | Treating duty lists as proof |
| Mid-career IC | End-to-end ownership, cross-functional influence, system scale, repeated outcomes | Assuming ambition requires management |
| Senior or Staff IC | Domain authority, leverage through standards and mentoring, ambiguous problem ownership, org-wide impact | Inflating strategy claims without personal attribution |
| Manager | Team outcomes, hiring and development, resource decisions, business delivery | Crediting team results without leadership mechanisms |
| Career changer or Oleh | Transferable problem patterns, work authorization, local equivalence, clear transition thesis | Deducting for unfamiliar employers or non-Israeli institutions |

---

## Job Match Mode: Two Scores

When a job description is provided, produce two separate scores:

**Score 1: CV Quality (0-100)** — full 100-point model above.

**Score 2: Match to Role (0-100):**

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Mandatory requirements met | 40 |
| Keywords present (truthfully) | 20 |
| Proof of execution and scale | 20 |
| Seniority alignment | 10 |
| Constraints (location, language, domain) | 10 |

Accompany with:
- Keyword comparison table: **Required Keywords | User Evidence | Rephrasing Strategy**
- Explanation from a cynical hiring manager's perspective
- "Development Opportunity" note for each gap — never invent experience
