# CV Review Guidelines

> Reference document for the CV Review Gem. Use throughout analysis, optimization, and feedback.

---

## 1. What to Check in Every CV

Evaluate across these eight dimensions for every analysis:

| Dimension | What to look for |
|---|---|
| **Readability and quick scan** | Is the top third clear and scannable? Does it follow the F-pattern: top line, left spine, recent roles first? |
| **Focus** | Is it immediately obvious what role this person fits? Is there one coherent role identity? |
| **Impact** | Are achievements backed by numbers, KPIs, scale, timelines, and measurable results? |
| **Experience** | Is every role explained through action, personal ownership, and outcome — not a list of duties? |
| **Skills and education** | Organized, relevant, matched to the target role? No exhaustive padding? |
| **ATS integrity** | Text-based, single-column, no tables/images/graphics, contact info not trapped in header/footer? |
| **Language** | Spelling, grammar, phrasing consistency, proofreading quality? |
| **Credibility** | Employment continuity, job title logic, timeline gaps, overall narrative coherence? |

## 2. ATS and Israeli Market Rules

### What harms ATS parsing (always flag)
- Multi-column layouts
- Tables for any content other than simple data
- Text inside images or graphics
- Contact info placed in document headers or footers
- Icons, skill bars, progress circles
- Canva-style designs with decorative elements
- Scanned or image-based PDFs

### Israeli ATS landscape
- **Greenhouse** — fully parses Hebrew. Fails on: images, complex tables, headers/footers with contact info.
- **Spark Hire / Comeet** — supports RTL languages, keyword-based search. Same formatting risks apply.
- **Workday** — text extraction with keyword matching. Bilingual files increase parsing risk.

### Bilingual CV rules
- Duplicate only critical role titles and skill labels in both languages.
- Do not duplicate full bullet points — it wastes space and confuses parsers.
- Keep clean, text-based formatting throughout.
- Recommend separate Hebrew and English master versions when possible.

### Keyword strategy
- Concentrate must-have keywords in: Summary, Skills section, and most recent role.
- Integrate them naturally into accomplishment bullets.
- Do not stuff. After coverage is established, repetition adds no value.
- Mirror exact job description terminology (e.g., "React Native" not "React for mobile").

## 3. The 20 Quality Rules

| Rule | Standard |
|---|---|
| R1 | Up to 5 years of experience: ideally 1 page |
| R2 | 10+ years of experience: up to 2 pages |
| R3 | No filler, no generic soft skills lists ("team player," "results-driven") |
| R4 | Use relevant keywords from the target job description |
| R5 | Achievements backed by numbers wherever possible |
| R6 | For management roles: team size, budget owned, KPIs |
| R7 | For technical roles: stack, systems, scale |
| R8 | Simple, consistent font throughout |
| R9 | Bold and highlights used sparingly and intentionally |
| R10 | Short, strong Summary at the top (2-4 lines) |
| R11 | Most recent experience at the top |
| R12 | Explain job hops where possible — do not leave the reader guessing |
| R13 | No Canva-style design unless the role is design |
| R14 | Clean, single-column layout |
| R15 | Clear visual hierarchy: sections, titles, bullets |
| R16 | No photo (bias risk and ATS failure risk) |
| R17 | ATS-clean: no tables, no graphics, no text boxes |
| R18 | Junior candidates do not need 2 pages |
| R19 | Most impactful achievement in the first bullet of each role |
| R20 | State work authorization or citizenship if relevant to the role |

## 4. Edge Cases

| Scenario | Approach |
|---|---|
| **Junior candidate** | Look for projects, tools, courses, volunteering, and proof of capability — not commercial tenure |
| **Manager** | Look for team size, KPI results, processes built, budget owned, and cross-functional impact |
| **Technical IC** | Look for stack ownership, production systems, scale, and end-to-end delivery |
| **Career changer** | Look for a clear, explicit transition story and transferable evidence |
| **Female IC (Israeli market)** | Watch for modesty traps: "assisted," "helped," "was involved in" — reframe into ownership language. "Sole IC responsible for X" and "Primary owner of Y" are high-signal phrases |
| **Very weak CV** | Give triage: identify the top 3 things that must be fixed before anything else |
| **Over-designed CV** | Recommend building a clean ATS version in parallel |
| **Short tenures** | Note the pattern factually, do not judge |
| **Missing LinkedIn** | Recommend adding and maintaining a consistent, updated profile |
| **Oleh / career changer** | Note transferable competencies. Do not penalize for unfamiliar institutions, non-Israeli experience, or unexplained gaps |
| **Israeli military service** | Translate relevant service experience into business competencies. Do not award points for unit prestige, rank, or combat status |

## 5. Rewriting Rules

### When rewriting is requested:

1. Write a sharp, one-line **Headline**: role identity + signature value.
2. Write a **Summary** of 3-4 lines:
   - Target identity and specialization
   - Signature strength tied to an outcome
   - Scale and complexity handled
3. Provide up to **6 rewritten bullet points** per role.
4. Each bullet formula: **Action Verb + Task/Context + Result**

### Verb guidance

**Strong ownership verbs to use:**
- Initiative: Owned, Led, Drove, Spearheaded, Initiated, Championed
- Execution: Designed, Implemented, Engineered, Developed, Shipped, Executed, Refactored, Optimized
- Collaboration: Partnered with, Aligned, Facilitated, Mentored

**Low-power constructions to eliminate:**
- "Helped with," "Assisted in," "Participated in," "Was involved in," "Responsible for supporting"

### The No-Inflation Rule
- You may rephrase titles for clarity (e.g., "Lead Developer" to "Senior Software Engineer" if the JD uses that term).
- You must NEVER elevate seniority (e.g., do not change "Manager" to "Director" or "Analyst" to "Lead").
- If no numbers exist, do not fabricate. Write a specific, strong version without invented metrics.
- Anchor achievements in team and business outcomes — not self-praise.

### Example transformation

**Before (weak):**
> Responsible for customer onboarding and worked with Sales and Product.

**After (strong):**
> Owned onboarding for 38 B2B accounts across Israel and Europe; partnered with Sales and Product to cut median time-to-value from 21 to 13 days.

If numbers cannot be disclosed:
> Owned onboarding for a multi-market B2B portfolio; redesigned the Sales-to-Product handoff and reduced time-to-value by more than one-third.

## 6. Israeli Market Context

### Communication style
- Israeli workplace culture values directness (dugri), informality, and confident, specific claims.
- Avoid: ceremonial prose, excessive hedging, inflated superlatives ("outstanding," "passionate," "dynamic").
- Write: direct, evidence-backed, action-oriented bullets that claim ownership without apology.

### Gender dynamics for female IC candidates
- Women are disproportionately represented in support, HR, and marketing roles in Israeli tech — recruiters may default to lower-status role expectations unless the CV counters this explicitly.
- High-signal phrases: "IC engineer on a team of X," "Hands-on data analyst," "End-to-end product owner (without direct reports)."
- Explicit framing of autonomy: "Sole IC responsible for X," "Primary owner of Y subsystem," "Trusted go-to person for Z domain."
- Avoid over-indexing on education and certificates — this reinforces a "good student, low power" stereotype.

### Academic and military signals
- In high-tech and startups: demonstrated execution outweighs degrees beyond the first screening pass.
- Prestigious Israeli institutions (Technion, Hebrew University, Tel Aviv University, Weizmann) can be named succinctly, then subordinated to recent impact.
- Military service: translate relevant experience into business competencies. Do not include unit prestige, rank, or combat status.

## 7. LinkedIn Audit Guidelines

When reviewing a LinkedIn profile or "About" section, suggest:

**Headline:**
- Should state: current role/archetype + signature value + specialization
- Not: job title only (e.g., "Software Engineer at Company X")
- Yes: "Senior Backend Engineer | Scaling distributed systems | Python, Go, Kubernetes"

**About section:**
- Mirror the CV summary: target identity, scope, signature evidence, specialty
- First 2-3 lines are visible before "see more" — front-load the strongest claim
- End with a clear signal of what opportunities the person is open to

**Experience:**
- Match titles and dates exactly to the CV — inconsistency damages credibility
- Add at least one accomplishment bullet per role — not just a description
- Include skills tags on each role for recruiter search visibility

**Skills section:**
- Prioritize skills that match the target role
- Remove outdated or irrelevant skills that dilute keyword relevance

**General:**
- Profile photo: professional, clear headshot (LinkedIn is not an ATS — photos are expected here)
- Recommendations: even one strong recommendation significantly increases credibility
- Keep it updated — outdated profiles reduce trust
